1. Household Registration & Membership Rules

  • Self-Service Adult Registration: Every adult member registers personally with their own phone OTP. A Head of Family cannot input personal records on behalf of other adults without independent self-service verification.
  • One Identity, One Headship: Under Invariant #6, an individual identity may head at most one registered Family. Duplicate headship attempts are detected and flagged.
  • Permanent Family ID: The issued Family ID (AGR-[PINCODE]-[SEQ]) is permanent and does not change even if the household changes residences.
  • Registration Friction Payment: The nominal ₹1 Registration Payment exists solely as a fraud friction mechanism to verify that a genuine human is completing registration.

2. The 18 Gotras of Maharaja Agrasen

In accordance with Invariant #4, the Gotra belongs to the Family, is chosen at creation from the eighteen traditional Gotras, and is immutable thereafter:

Garg (गर्ग) Goyal (गोयल) Goyan (गोयन) Bansal (बंसल) Kansal (कंसल) Singhal (सिंघल) Jindal (जिंदल) Tingal (टिंगल) Mohan (मोहन) Dharan (धारण) Madhukul (मधुकुल) Bindal (बिंदल) Mittal (मित्तल) Tayal (तायल) Mangal (मंगल) Airan (ऐरण) Nangal (नांगल) Kuchhal (कुच्छल)

3. Community Noticeboard Standards

Members may post to community boards (Shok Sandesh, Business Listings, Events). All posts are subject to:

  • No Personal Phone Leakage: Notice text cannot include personal phone numbers to circumvent phone privacy protections. Only official business listings may publish their verified commercial contact numbers.
  • Commercial Notice Posting Fee: Commercial notices require a nominal ₹49 posting fee to curb unsolicited spam and bulk advertising.
  • Shok Sandesh (Obituaries): Publishing an obituary announces a passing to the community; it does not automatically archive the deceased member's directory record without family confirmation.

4. Moderation & Suspensions

To maintain a safe, respectful environment for all member families:

  • Distinct Family Reports: A notice is hidden only if it receives reports from multiple distinct households, preventing bad-faith harassment by a single party.
  • Transparent Suspensions: If a member's posting capability is temporarily suspended for policy violations, the suspension is fully visible to the member along with reasons. Shadowbanning is strictly forbidden.
  • Emergency Protection: Blood SOS emergency alerts cannot be hidden by member reports, preventing malicious silencing of medical requests.

5. Event Passes & Gate Verification

  • Free Event Passes: Event passes for community functions are non-commercial and never carry a charge.
  • Offline Verification: Passes are digitally signed by the server and verified cryptographically at venue gates without requiring active cellular connectivity.
  • Headcount for Minors: Minors enter on the Head of Family's pass as an aggregate headcount, ensuring complete privacy from presence tracking.

6. Human Officer Review & Appeals

Every automated platform decision (including image screening rejections, duplicate registration flags, or content suspensions) carries a right of human review by the Grievance & Verification Officer:

  • Officer Email: help.agrawal.app@gmail.com
  • Response SLA: Within 30 days
  • The Officer possesses the authority to override erroneous automated classifications and reinstate affected accounts.
