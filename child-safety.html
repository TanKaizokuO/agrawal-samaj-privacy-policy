Child Safety & CSAE Prevention

Child Safety Standards

Agrawal Samaj has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our standards, how anyone can report a concern, and what we do when we receive one.

1. Zero Tolerance

Agrawal Samaj prohibits any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children. This applies to every part of the app, including profiles, photos, family records, notices, business listings, events and any messages. Violations lead to immediate removal of the content, permanent removal of the account, and a report to the relevant authorities.

2. Prohibited Content & Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material.
  • Grooming, or any attempt to contact or build a relationship with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualising children in any photo, caption, notice or comment.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a child.
  • Trafficking of children, or promoting or facilitating it.
  • Using family, event or directory information to locate, track or target a child.

3. How the App Protects Children

  • Adults only: Only adults aged 18 and over can create an account. Every account is tied to a verified mobile number through OTP sign-in.
  • Verified members: Families are reviewed by community verifiers before they appear in the directory.
  • Limited data about minors: Minors cannot sign in. A minor appears only as part of their family record, added by the Head of Family, and at events minors are counted as a headcount on the Head of Family's pass rather than tracked individually.
  • Photo screening: Uploaded photos pass through automated safety screening for explicit content before they are shown, with human review by the Verification Officer.
  • No private phone numbers: Members' phone numbers are never shown in the directory or search, and notices cannot publish personal phone numbers.

4. How to Report

Anyone can report a child safety concern, whether or not they use the app:

  • In the app: Use the Report option on any notice or business listing and choose Inappropriate media or the closest reason, then describe the concern. Reports go to the Samaj Verification Officer.
  • By email: Write to help.agrawal.app@gmail.com with the subject Child Safety. Include what you saw, where in the app, and the member name or Family ID if you know it. Do not attach or forward abusive images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger: Call the police on 112 or Childline on 1098 first, then tell us.
  • To the authorities directly: You can also report online child sexual abuse material to India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in.

5. How We Respond

  • Child safety reports are handled ahead of all other reports and reviewed by a person, not only by automated systems.
  • Content that appears to be CSAM or to put a child at risk is removed or hidden from members as soon as we are aware of it.
  • Accounts responsible for CSAE are permanently removed, and the person is blocked from registering again.
  • We preserve the relevant records securely and only for as long as needed to support law enforcement, as required by law.
  • Blood SOS alerts and other emergency features are never used as a reason to delay action on a child safety report.

6. Legal Compliance & Reporting to Authorities

Agrawal Samaj complies with applicable child safety laws, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. Confirmed CSAM and child sexual exploitation are reported to the police and through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, and to other regional or national authorities where required. We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

7. Child Safety Contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM prevention is the Samaj Grievance & Verification Officer:

  • Email: help.agrawal.app@gmail.com (subject: Child Safety)
  • Response: Child safety reports are acknowledged as soon as possible, and within 24 hours.
