1. Google Play Console High-Level Questionnaire Answers

Google Play Console Question Platform Response Details & Context
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? Yes Data is collected strictly for core community directory, family verification, and emergency blood matching.
Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Yes All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS with TLS 1.3 protocol.
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Yes Self-service deletion in the app (Settings → Delete Account) and web portal at /delete-account.html.
Does your app target children under the age of 13? No Registration is restricted to adults (18+). Children are never assigned independent accounts.
Is your app committed to following the Google Play Families Policy? Not Applicable The app is for adult community members and not in the Designed for Families program.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing Declarations

Zero Commercial Sharing / Zero Data Sales
Personal data is never transferred or sold to data brokers, marketing affiliates, or advertising networks. Data is shared solely with critical infrastructure providers acting under written data processing contracts:
  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. (Payment Processor): Order reference, transaction amounts for statutory ₹1 payment processing.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Push): Phone numbers for one-time SMS verification and ephemeral device tokens for push alerts.
  • Amazon Web Services (Cloud Host): Encrypted database records stored in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1).
  • Sightengine (Content Moderation): Transient media screening to block abusive content.

4. Security Practices Declared in Play Console

  • Data is encrypted in transit: Yes. Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protects all data moving across the public network.
  • Data is encrypted at rest: Yes. PostgreSQL data tables and media objects in Amazon S3 use AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Independent Security Review: Architecture and data access controls are audited for compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play developer security guidelines.
