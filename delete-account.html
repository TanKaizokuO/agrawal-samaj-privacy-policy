Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Under the Google Play Developer Data Deletion Policy and Section 12(3) of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the absolute right to delete your account and request complete erasure of your personal data from the Agrawal Samaj Community App (app.agrawal.agrawal_samaj).

1. How to Delete Your Account from Within the App

If you have the Agrawal Samaj Android application installed on your device, you can trigger self-service account deletion directly in seconds:

  1. Open the Agrawal Samaj app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on your Profile Avatar in the top-right corner to open the Member Profile drawer.
  3. Select Settings & Preferences.
  4. Tap on Privacy & Data Consent.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account / Erasure.
  6. Verify your request with a one-time SMS OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
Instant Effect
Once OTP verification completes, your session is immediately invalidated, and your profile is purged from the Samaj community directory within seconds.

3. What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

In accordance with System Invariant #17 and DPDP Act Section 12(3), account erasure executes in two distinct stages:

Data Category Action on Deletion Timeframe
Public Directory Profile Name, Gotra, city, state, and community cards are completely wiped. Immediate (0-24 hrs)
Photographs & Media Profile pictures and uploaded images are removed from AWS S3 storage. Immediate
Phone Number & Auth Sign-in session invalidated, active auth tokens revoked. Immediate
Family Links & Household Link to Family record unlinked. If you were Head of Family, headship transfers to your Nominee. Immediate
Emergency Blood SOS Data Donor matching opt-in record and blood group link are removed. Immediate
Statutory Audit & Payment Logs ₹1 Registration transaction IDs and consent audit trails are retained in isolated cold storage for legal and tax compliance. 12 Months (Statutory)

4. Statutory Retention Requirements

Why do we retain audit and payment records for 12 months after profile deletion?

  • DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 8(3): Mandates that data fiduciaries maintain access-restricted processing logs for 12 months following account closure to assist statutory regulatory verification and grievance dispute audits.
  • Financial & Banking Regulations: Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directives and the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), digital payment gateway transactions (via Razorpay) require audit trails to prevent fraud and enable chargeback settlements.

These records are kept in encrypted, access-restricted cold storage and are permanently deleted once the statutory retention timeline concludes.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account after deletion?
No. Once account deletion is executed, your member record, directory profile, and family links are permanently purged. If you wish to rejoin the Samaj in the future, you will need to register as a new applicant with phone OTP verification.
What happens if I am the Head of Family?
Under System Invariant #5, if the Head of Family deletes their account or undergoes erasure, headship passes immediately to the designated adult Nominee. If no Nominee was specified, it transitions to the next-oldest adult Member in the household.
Will my community noticeboard posts also be deleted?
Under Invariant #17, your personal profile and identifiers are stripped. Public obituaries (Shok Sandesh) and community notices may remain as Historical Content to preserve the community archive, with all personal contact references expunged.
How long does it take for a web deletion request to be processed?
Web requests sent to the Grievance Officer (help.agrawal.app@gmail.com) are reviewed and processed within 30 calendar days as required by DPDP statutory timelines.
