3. Emergency Blood SOS & Health Data Safeguards

The Platform includes an emergency Blood SOS feature designed to mobilize donors within the Samaj during critical medical emergencies at hospitals.

Absolute Privacy Invariant: Blood Group is NEVER Displayed

In accordance with binding system invariant #20: Blood Group is never displayed to anyone under any circumstances.

  • Blood group is collected optionally from registered adults for the sole purpose of matching compatibility with Blood SOS emergencies.
  • It is not shown in the public directory, not shown to linked family members, and not shown to other community members.
  • A Blood SOS alert notifies matching donors via server-side push notifications without disclosing the medical history or identity of potential donors to the requester.
  • Members can toggle off Blood SOS participation at any time in their profile settings.

4. Contact Information & Phone Number Privacy

Unlike open social networks or unverified public directories, the Agrawal Samaj Platform is engineered with strict default contact privacy:

  • Zero Directory Exposure: A member's phone number is never published in the community directory, search results, or general profile previews.
  • Linked Family Audience: A member's phone number is visible only to verified adults within their own linked Family.
  • Friend Contact Share: When two members outside the same family establish mutual friendship, each member may individually choose to toggle "Contact Share" for phone or email. This is off by default, granular per field, and immediately revocable.
  • No Number Leaks via Contact Requests or Auto-Groups: In-app messaging, Gotra Auto-Groups, and Contact Requests use internal member handles and never expose actual telephone numbers.

5. Biometrics, Device Tokens & Local Hardware Security

The mobile application provides biometric app lock (fingerprint / face unlock) for member convenience and protection on shared mobile devices:

On-Device Biometric Isolation

Biometric verification is executed strictly on your local device through Android's native BiometricPrompt and hardware-backed Android Keystore:

  • Zero Transmission: Fingerprint and facial biometric data are processed within your device's hardware Secure Element (TEE) and are never transmitted to our backend servers or stored in any database.
  • Zero Access: The application receives only a cryptographic boolean confirmation from Android whether authentication succeeded.
  • Revocable: App lock can be enabled or disabled at any time in the app's Security Settings.

7. Granular Consent & Member Visibility Controls

In accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023, consent is collected on an itemised, freely given, specific, and informed basis. Members can manage four independent consent toggles:

  • Directory Listing Toggle: Dictates whether your profile appears in the verified Samaj member directory. (Revoking this toggle triggers membership withdrawal and profile erasure).
  • Blood SOS Notification Toggle: Controls whether you receive life-saving emergency donation alerts for compatible blood requests in your geographic zone.
  • Photo Visibility Toggle: Enables or disables display of your profile picture across community directory surfaces.
  • Birthday Board Toggle: Allows you to opt out of having your date of birth or wedding anniversary featured on the daily community greetings board.

8. Account Deletion & Two-Stage Data Erasure

We uphold the unconditional right of members to request erasure of their personal data under Section 12(3) of the DPDP Act and the Google Play Account Deletion Policy.

Two-Stage Erasure Architecture
  1. Stage 1 — Immediate Production Purge: All member-visible data (including your profile, name, photographs, directory cards, active device sessions, and push tokens) is immediately and permanently wiped from live databases and search indexes.
  2. Stage 2 — Statutory Log Retention: To comply with mandatory requirements under Indian law (including DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 8(3) requiring 1-year processing log retention and Indian tax/accounting record-keeping regulations), financial transaction records and immutable audit logs are transferred to isolated, restricted storage for 12 months, after which they are automatically and permanently destroyed.

To delete your account, you may use either method:

  • Inside the App: Navigate to Settings → Privacy & Consent → Delete Account / Erasure.
  • Via the Web: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Portal or email help.agrawal.app@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy (Protection of Minors Under 18)

The Agrawal Samaj Platform is strictly designed for and directed to adults aged 18 and older:

  • No Minor Accounts: Individuals under 18 years of age are legally ineligible to self-register or maintain an independent user account.
  • Event Headcount Only: Minors accompanying adult members to community functions are recorded solely as an anonymous headcount on the Head of Family's non-transferable event pass. No digital profiles, photos, or tracking identifiers are created for minors.
  • No Minor Tracking: The platform contains no behavioral analytics, profiling, or targeted alerts directed toward children.

10. Information Security & Data Safeguards

We employ defense-in-depth technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of data transmitted between the Android mobile application, web portal, and backend servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database stores, media caches, and cloud snapshots are encrypted with AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict principle of least privilege applies. Production database access requires multi-factor authentication and is restricted to essential maintenance.
  • Tamper-Evident Audit Trails: Sensitive operations (including duplicate identity reviews, image moderation appeals, and manual archival entries) write append-only, immutable logs.

11. Core Platform Privacy Invariants

The architecture of the Agrawal Samaj Platform is governed by 25 immutable invariants, codified into software rules to guarantee member safety:

Invariant #16 No Public Phone Numbers

A member's phone number is visible only to verified linked family, or to a friend who explicitly accepted a Contact Share. No directory setting ever exposes it publicly.

Invariant #20 Blood Group Never Displayed

Blood Group is collected solely to match urgent Blood SOS alerts to consenting donors. It is never displayed to anyone, including family and requesters.

Invariant #7 Adult-Only Register

Only adults exist in the register. Minors hold no individual accounts or directory entries and appear only as an anonymous headcount on event passes.

Invariant #17 Immediate Profile Erasure

Account erasure immediately purges all public and member-facing data, holding only mandatory statutory audit records in restricted storage for legal periods.

Invariant #11 Automated Media Screening

Explicit imagery is refused at upload via automated screening, with Officer review for appeals. Non-consensual content is permanently prohibited.

Invariant #18 Human Grievance Officer

The Grievance Officer is a published, named human with a mandatory 30-day resolution SLA and power to overturn automated determinations.
